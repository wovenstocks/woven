# Code of conduct

Woven contributors are expected to keep collaboration professional, specific,
and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss code and decisions without personal attacks or harassment.
- Welcome questions and give actionable, evidence-based feedback.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licenses, and responsible disclosure.
- Disclose conflicts of interest that could affect technical or security decisions.
- Prioritize user safety over speed, publicity, or financial incentives.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deceptive security claims, market
manipulation, deliberate exposure of secrets, and public disclosure of an
unresolved vulnerability are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation,
or take other proportionate action when this policy is violated. Sensitive conduct
reports should be sent privately through the maintainer contact options provided
by the repository or its GitHub organization. Security reports must follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
